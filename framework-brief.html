StratXe · Portfolio & project modules

Portfolio, programme and project: framework, rules and modules

The structure for the StratXe portfolio layer: the hierarchy, the Triple-P rules that define each level, the components under every area, and the module and tab map that feeds the mockup.

01 · The framework

The hierarchy

Two decisions shape the hierarchy: APP and AOP are split into distinct bands, and portfolio definition is elevated directly under SP (it can start as soon as outcomes exist), while portfolio management runs in parallel with strategic planning rather than above it. Linked responsibilities with a continuous feedback loop, not a strict containment triangle.

The fork

Portfolio definition triggers a fork per outcome. Strategic → strategic projects back into decision-making, or cascades to APP → programmes → AOP. Operational → the AOP placeholder, allocated after APP focus to programmes → operational projects. Both converge on programmes → projects → delivery, then benefit evidence flows back up.

Decision point. Where the "strategic thinking" step sits: a step before framework setup (after diagnosis) is recommended, so outcomes can be defined and linked to portfolios before the full scorecard is built. This adjusts the AI diagnostic slightly: outcomes as goalposts first, scorecard second.

02 · Triple-P rules

What defines each level

The rules that separate the three levels. Where they differ is the whole point.

Change/closureRestructured/closed when mandate endsCloses when benefit realisedCloses at deliverable acceptance
Programme scope and dates. Yes to scope, qualified on dates. A programme has a defined objective and scope (its shared benefit); its dates are derived from its projects' coordination window. Bounded, but not date-first like a project. A folder of unrelated projects is not a programme.
Portfolio programme vs APP programme. No: store them separately. Budget/APP programme = a planning/budget classification (today, internally, a stand-in for departments). Delivery programme = coordinated projects for a benefit. Keep budget-programme classification separate from delivery-programme membership. This also fixes "programmes act as departments."

Nesting rules

1Portfolio may contain sub-portfolios, delivery programmes, and (guardrailed) projects.
2Programme sits under a portfolio or sub-portfolio; may contain sub-programmes.
3Project sits under a programme; directly under a portfolio only where guardrails allow.
4Nothing authorised sits outside a portfolio; ownership starts at the portfolio.
5BAU/operations is a separate layer, not a project (tenders, marketing & comms).
6Guardrails define which small/local projects may bypass portfolio governance.

MVP build order

The MVP is the foundations: define portfolios, structure them into programmes and projects, apply the governance layer. Not the full workflow.

  1. Strategic thinking → outcomes → portfolio definition.
  2. Portfolio composition + charter + roadmap.
  3. Programmes/projects redesign (business case, types, dependencies).
  4. Governance layer (forums, rules, approvals): foundations only.
  5. Benefits realisation + performance roll-up.
