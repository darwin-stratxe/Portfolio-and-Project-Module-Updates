# Framework breakdown: components under each area

Components under each framework area · 16 September 2026

For each area of the framework, the sub-components that sit underneath it, with a one-line definition and the public/private sector overlay. Areas follow the hierarchy in `framework-design.md`.

---

## 1. Strategy

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Vision & mission | The direction being set |
| Strategic themes | The enduring areas of focus strategy is organised around |
| Strategic assumptions | If/then beliefs the strategy depends on; tracked and tested over time |

*Public/private:* themes framed as service mandates (public) or market positions (private).

## 2. Strategic Plan (SP)

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Multi-year outcomes | What the organisation intends to achieve over the cycle (5-year), defined as goalposts |
| Outcome grouping | Grouping outcomes by focus / impact / resource allocation (CIPC groups by focus area); this grouping becomes the basis of a portfolio |
| Multi-year roadmap | The horizon: milestones, capabilities, sequencing |
| Scorecard | The measurable translation of outcomes (built *after* outcomes are set) |

*Public/private:* public uses focus-area grouping aligned to mandate; private groups by strategic bet / business line.

## 3. Portfolio definition

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Portfolio composition | Which outcomes/initiatives/decisions/cross-functional elements form each portfolio |
| Portfolio roadmap | The multi-year roadmap for the portfolio, pulled from the SP horizon |
| Strategic/operational fork | Classifies each portfolio as inherently strategic or operational |
| Investment thesis & indicative funding | Why invest, and the indicative envelope and capacity |
| Portfolio charter | Stable ID, sponsor, forum, decision rights, boundaries, review date |

*Public/private:* public portfolios often outcome/mandate-based; private often capability/market-based. Do not create a portfolio just because a department exists.

## 4. Strategic planning: APP

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Annual focus | The one-year slice of the roadmap |
| Outputs & targets | Annual outputs and targets tied to SP outcomes |
| Programme allocation | Outcomes/outputs allocated to programmes for the year |
| Prioritisation model | How this year's focus is prioritised across portfolios |
| Forward projections | Multi-year projection of annual targets |

*Public/private:* public = DPME/PFMA APP structure; private = annual business plan.

## 5. Strategic planning: AOP

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Departmental activities | Operational activities and outputs beyond APP |
| Resource commitments | People, capital and external resources committed by department |
| Operational budgets | Budgets and budget-programme classification (kept separate from delivery programmes) |
| Confirmed allocations | Allocations confirmed by the relevant resource/financial authority |

*Public/private:* public keeps budget-programme vs implementation-programme distinct; private lighter on budget classification.

## 6. Portfolio management (parallel governance layer)

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Demand inflow | Intake from SP, APP, AOP and portfolio-originated opportunities; per-portfolio intake rules |
| Business case / new request assessment | Submission and assessment of a business case; who may submit is a governance rule |
| Governance forums & rules | Configurable forums and decision rights per portfolio; centralised or decentralised |
| Backlog | Retained, unscheduled demand |
| Scenario analysis | Alternative investment mixes / hypotheses tested before selection |
| Prioritisation | Ranking that feeds authorised work into programmes/projects |
| Authorisation / investment | Recorded decision, funding ceiling, conditions; strategic vs operational, billable vs non-billable |
| Oversight | Exceptions, forecast changes, risk, stale evidence, condition compliance |
| Support escalation | Accountable person at each layer can resolve or push support needs up without waiting for a forum |

*Public/private:* public adds funding-release gates (funds committed but not disbursed until conditions met); private adds commercial-return criteria.

## 7. Programme & project management

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Programme definition | Objective, scope, coordinated benefit; delivery programme (not a department) |
| Business case (project) | Aligns a project to a fund/outcome; billable / non-billable / BAU |
| Project definition | Deliverable, scope, fixed start/end |
| Dependencies | Tracked within and across programmes and portfolios |
| Roll-up / roll-down health | Cascade health check up and down the layers |
| Programme–department linkage | Projects still link to departments/business units as a reporting dimension |

*Public/private:* same delivery mechanics; project types differ (public service delivery vs private billable/commercial).

## 8. Operations management / BAU

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Day-to-day operations | Recurring work outside the change workflow |
| Time & workflow tracking | Time tracking extended beyond projects to ops |
| Ops-vs-project allocation | e.g. 80% ops / 20% projects, managed as structured allocations |
| BAU targets | Targets on continuous delivery (tenders, marketing & comms), not projects |
| Capacity visibility | BAU capacity/cost/constraints made visible to portfolio decisions |

*Public/private:* AIA-type orgs run projects-heavy (80/20 the other way); general orgs ops-heavy.

## 9. Resources

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| People / capacity | Skill pools and capacity by period; can sit at portfolio or programme level |
| Capital / investment | Capital allocation treated as a resource allocation |
| Strategic (thinking) capacity | Deliberate allocation of thinking capacity across areas |
| External resources | Externally funded or externally provided resources |
| Allocation & conflicts | Managing contention across areas |

## 10. Financials

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Capital allocation performance | Financial performance from a capital-allocation view |
| Revenue generation | Revenue tracking (to be added; current view is capital only) |
| Funding status | Committed vs disbursed, per public-sector release rules |

## 11. Performance & benefits realisation

| Component | Definition |
|---|---|
| Portfolio KPIs | Portfolio-level measures; can inherit from SP or be defined separately (execution-level, not just targets) |
| Data-linked KPIs | KPIs data-linked down to projects and up to individual performance contracts |
| Benefits definition | Baseline, target, due date defined up front at project setup |
| Post-closure benefit tracking | Benefits tracked after project closure, then rolled up to programme → portfolio |
| Benefit-to-fund test | Whether the realised benefit justifies the portfolio's fund allocation |

*Public/private:* public benefits = service/equity outcomes; private = value/margin/growth.

---

## 12. Cross-cutting design principles

- **Governance without delay**: pre-push decisions before forums; SteerCo only for final decision or intervention.
- **Separate permission to investigate from permission to implement**: a small discovery allocation can precede full investment.
- **Configurable governance depth** by organisational maturity: some orgs run portfolio-only; others add programme-level governance.
- **Same architecture, different layers** for public / private / AIA-internal clients.
